## Original literal gaps

The policy helper was unused. Static heap-based allocation had no observed-resource scheduler, durable workload selection/checkpoint pauses or adaptive actual capture budgets.

## Implemented behavior and integration

Bound managed/OS memory, thermal, power/battery and explicit unknown observations to admission/hysteresis. ResourceCheckpointGate rechecks 021 storage and propagates typed pauses through optional stages to durable job checkpoints. Persisted version 1 COMPACT uses depth 256/texture 512, at most 6 views/50,000 samples; STANDARD uses 384/768, at most 8 views/100,000 samples only with known sufficient headroom. Actual DenseWorkload/DensePreview consume limits before allocation and OpenCV uses one native thread. Resume reproduces the same plan or pauses. Geometry/appearance have separate domain/version identities so appearance-only changes preserve geometry while full selection remains immutable. Bounded accepted-surface replay gets additional 24 MiB native working and24 MiB peak disk for a 12 MiB maximum encoded checkpoint; old retained files are already measured, never assumed reclaimable. Actual camera analysis reduces dimensions/cadence under pressure and waits for healthy hysteresis before raising them. Quantities are conservative estimates.
