# 055 pure topology diagnostics

`analyzeMeshTopology(mesh,settings)` is header-only, deterministic, bounded by
maxVertices/maxFaces/maxIntersectionCandidates, and cancels at bounded work.
No vertex/face mutation or automatic repair occurs. Face IDs are input indices;
all report defects retain those IDs. Duplicate faces are sorted-index duplicates
excluding the first face. Degenerates use scale-relative double-area tolerance;
open boundaries are counted as evidence, not declared invalid or closed.

MeshTopologyReport includes degenerateFaces, duplicateFaces, windingConflictFaces,
nonmanifoldFaces, edge/vertex counts, connected face components, boundaryLoops
and boundaryChains. Broad-phase sweep AABBs bound exact triangle candidate tests.
Long-double predicates classify definite intersections and coplanar positive-area
overlaps separately from tolerance-band ambiguousPairs. Shared-index adjacency is not skipped: expected edge/vertex contact is
reported as ambiguous, while interior crossings and positive-area overlaps remain
definite even when faces share vertices. The budget counts every broad-phase
pair examined, including Y/Z rejects, and checks cancellation every64 pairs. A candidate-budget exhaustion flag means
intersection diagnosis is incomplete, never zero defects.

Tolerance scales with input bounding diagonal. Arbitrary scale remains arbitrary;
no physical error estimate or watertight certification is inferred. Consumers
must keep original observed geometry and provenance and report uncertain results.
